# AF SCOOTERS

**Electric scooter shop, repair workshop and custom lithium battery manufacturer in Spain.**

AF SCOOTERS has operated since March 2017, with physical locations in Castellón de la Plana and Borriana. We serve customers throughout Spain and the European Union through our online store.

## What we do

- Electric scooter repair and maintenance
- Custom-made lithium battery manufacturing
- Batteries to increase electric scooter range
- Electric scooter sales
- Batteries, spare parts and accessories
- Technical support for individual and business customers

## Speed and autonomy batteries

AF SCOOTERS manufactures custom lithium batteries under the **INFINITA** brand, adapted to the voltage, capacity and configuration required by each electric scooter.
